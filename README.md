# LawConnect (NyaySaathi - न्याय साथी)
### Enterprise LegalTech Platform for Indian Citizens & Advocates

> Strictly compliant with **Bar Council of India (BCI) Rule 36** • Bridging the gap between 5.0+ Crore pending court cases and thousands of competent junior advocates.

---

## 🌟 Real-World Project Features

1. **Exact Reference Color Palette**:
   - Deep Legal Crimson (`#A6261B`)
   - Warm Sand / Cream Legal Paper Background (`#FAF6F0`)
   - Seafoam / Mint Green (`#E6F5F2`)
   - Deep Forest Charcoal / Teal (`#112321`)
   - Warm Coral Gradient (`#CF3B2B` → `#E26D54`)
   - Dark Bronze / Olive (`#443A2C`)

2. **Live Legal Activity Ticker**:
   - Real-time marquee broadcasting live dispute activity across Indian courts (Pune MahaRERA, Chandigarh Sec 138, New Delhi Cyber Fraud, Bengaluru Employment Dues).

3. **Interactive Indian Court & Limitation Period Calculator**:
   - Checks whether a citizen's dispute is within statutory limitation (Limitation Act 1963, NI Act 1881, Consumer Protection Act 2019, RERA 2016).
   - Recommends proper judicial forums (District Commission, JMIC, Adjudicating Officer).

4. **Free Indian Statutory Legal Notice Generator**:
   - Generates authentic Indian Advocate Letterhead notices with Bar Council stamps and statutory 15-day / 30-day demand clauses for Cheque Dishonour, Consumer Refunds, and Security Deposit claims.

5. **Virtual Consultation Chamber (Privileged Video Link)**:
   - Live consultation room simulator with active timer, advocate video stream, client webcam inset, mic/video toggles, and collaborative legal strategy notes scratchpad.

6. **BCI Verified Advocate Full Profiles**:
   - Complete chamber view with Bar Council ID, State Bar Council, law school alma mater (NLSIU, NLU Delhi, GLC Mumbai), practicing courts, and landmark milestone resolutions.

7. **Bilingual Support (English / हिंदी)**:
   - Instant 1-click toggle translating key headers and CTAs into authentic legal Hindi (न्याय साथी, 3 आसान चरण, युवा वकीलों के लिए, आम नागरिकों के लिए).

8. **Citizen Case Tracking Portal & Document Vault**:
   - Lifecycle timeline tracking, AES-256 encrypted legal proof storage, and live privileged chat with assigned counsel.

9. **Junior Lawyer Digital Chamber**:
   - Live lead feed of unrepresented citizens (*Direct Leads*), instant case acceptance, court cause list calendar, and 100% earnings retention.
